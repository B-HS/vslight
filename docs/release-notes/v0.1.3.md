VSLight v0.1.3 fixes the volume percentage field in the menu bar window.

## Fixes

- The percentage now follows the volume while the field is focused. Moving the slider, applying a preset, or changing the volume from anywhere else ends editing and shows the current value.
- Fixed the volume jumping back to the old number when the field lost focus after the slider had been moved.
- Clicking the percentage and leaving it unchanged no longer rounds the volume to the displayed digits.
- No audio driver changes. The v0.1.1 driver remains compatible, so upgrading the app does not require reinstalling the driver.

## Installation

1. Download `VSLight-0.1.3.zip`.
2. Move `VSLight.app` to `/Applications`.
3. Open the app.

If you already installed the v0.1.1 driver, keep it. Reinstall the driver only if the app reports that it is missing.

## Requirements

- macOS 27.0 or later
